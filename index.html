The Story

Forged in Queens, Crowned in Barcelona

Herb Perez's path to Olympic gold was anything but straight. Growing up in a Puerto Rican family in the tough neighborhoods of Queens, NY, he learned early that survival required more than talent — it required an unshakeable will.

After a heartbreaking loss at the national championships, Herb was approached by Harvey Berkey — a businessman with no martial arts background but a proven system for winning. What followed was a complete transformation: no excuses, no shortcuts, just a relentless pursuit of excellence.

"Taekwondo is a microcosm of life, and life reflects the lessons learned on the mat."

Today, Herb channels that hard-won wisdom into coaching, speaking, and books that have helped thousands of athletes, parents, and professionals unlock their full potential.
